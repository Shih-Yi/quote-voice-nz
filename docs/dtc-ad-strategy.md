## NZ Market Notes

### Platform Priority
1. **Facebook/Instagram** — Primary. NZ tradies are active on Facebook. Trade groups, marketplace, and Reels are high-traffic touchpoints. Allocate 70% of budget here.
2. **Google Search** — Secondary. High-intent buyers searching "quoting app nz", "tradify alternative". Allocate 30% of budget here for Phase 1. Scale when CPL is proven.
3. **TikTok** — Monitor only. Growing in NZ but unproven for B2B tradie audience. Revisit at 6 months.

### Tone and Creative Guidelines
- **Understated wins.** NZ tradies see through hype instantly. "Pretty bloody good" > "REVOLUTIONARY"
- **Authentic > polished.** UGC-style, on-site footage outperforms studio creative for this audience
- **Kiwi vocabulary matters.** Use "tradie", "sparky", "chippy", "the tools", "ute", "on-site", "GST", "arvo" where natural
- **Show the product.** A screen recording of the actual 60-second flow is more convincing than any claim
- **Real NZ locations.** Canterbury, Auckland, Wellington — specific is more believable than generic NZ stock

### Seasonal Budget Guidance
| Season | NZ Dates | Trade Activity | Messaging |
|---|---|---|---|
| Peak (Construction) | Sept–April | Very high | Speed / win more jobs |
| Off-peak (Winter) | May–Aug | Lower | Admin / catch-up / planning |
| Tax time | March–April | Admin-heavy | GST / compliance / quoting tools |

**Budget recommendation:** Increase by 30% Sept–Nov (spring construction rush). Reduce by 20% June–July (winter slow).

### Compliance Notes
- All claims must be substantiated per NZ Advertising Standards Authority (ASA) rules
- "60 seconds" claim must be demonstrable — do not publish until product can consistently deliver this
- Do not use competitor logos in ads without fair use justification
- "Free" must be genuinely free — no hidden credit card requirement during waitlist phase
- Testimonials must be from real NZ users — do not fabricate even for pre-launch

### Creative Fatigue Warning
- NZ audiences are small (50K–200K per targeting set)
- Creative fatigue hits in 2–3 weeks at $30–50/day
- Plan: minimum 2 new creative variations ready every fortnight
- Monitor frequency metric weekly — pause any ad set > 3.5 frequency on cold traffic

## Remotion Video Specs

### Template: "60-Second Hook" (Multi-Format)
**Compositions needed:**
- Horizontal (16:9) — 1920×1080 — YouTube, desktop feed
- Vertical (9:16) — 1080×1920 — Stories, Reels
- Square (1:1) — 1080×1080 — Feed posts

**Dynamic variables:**
- `{{trade}}`: Trade type (plumber, sparky, builder, landscaper)
- `{{hook}}`: Hook text (max 8 words)
- `{{time_saved}}`: Time saved claim (e.g., "60 seconds")
- `{{cta}}`: Button text (e.g., "Get early access free")
- `{{logo}}`: ChurQuote logo path
- `{{brand_primary}}`: #6366F1
- `{{brand_cta}}`: #6366F1
- `{{product_screenshot}}`: Quote PDF screenshot path

**Animation sequence:**
1. [0–3s]: Hook text animates in — bold, centred, white on dark — snappy entrance (200ms)
2. [3–8s]: Problem visual (tradie at desk at night) with text overlay
3. [8–18s]: Phone screen recording — voice → quote appears
4. [18–25s]: Quote PDF close-up with GST breakdown visible
5. [25–30s]: CTA card — logo, offer text, URL, button

**Design tokens:**
- Primary: #6366F1 (Indigo 500)
- CTA: #6366F1
- Secondary: #10B981 (Emerald 500)
- Background: #111827 (Slate 900) for ads — dark creates contrast
- Font: Bold for hooks (≥ 48px), Regular for body (24px)
- Motion: Snappy cuts (200ms), no slow cross-fades
- Safe zones: Keep all text within 80% of frame

**Trade variations to produce:**
- plumber, sparky/electrician, builder, landscaper, painter
- 5 trades × 3 formats = 15 video compositions per campaign
