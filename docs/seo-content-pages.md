## How to Fill In Your Plumbing Quote

### Step 1: Start with your details
Fill in your business name, NZBN number, phone number, and email. If you're GST-registered, include your GST number. This is a legal requirement for GST-registered businesses quoting over $50.

### Step 2: Add client and job details
Client name, job address, and contact phone number. Include a brief one-sentence scope of work — "Supply and install replacement hot water cylinder, disconnect and remove old unit."

### Step 3: List your materials
Go through each part you'll need. Be specific — vague line items lead to scope disputes. Include quantities and per-unit cost. Link to your supplier price list where possible.

### Step 4: Add your labour
Estimate your hours. Add a line for your call-out fee separately — this protects you if the job turns out to be simpler than expected.

### Step 5: Calculate GST
If quoting GST-exclusive: add a line "GST (15%)" as your subtotal × 0.15.
If quoting GST-inclusive: note "Prices include GST" at the top.

> **Quick NZ GST tip:** GST-exclusive total × 1.15 = GST-inclusive total. GST-inclusive total ÷ 1.15 = GST-exclusive total.

### Step 6: Set a validity period
Standard in NZ is 30 days. Material prices change — protect yourself with a clear expiry date.

### Step 7: Send it fast
First to quote wins. If you can send the quote the same day (or before you leave the site), your win rate improves significantly.

## GST on Plumbing Quotes: Inclusive vs Exclusive — What to Use

GST handling is one of the most common errors on NZ trade quotes. Here's when to use each:

### GST Exclusive (+ GST)
**Best for:** Quoting business clients (companies, property managers, developers)
**Format:** Subtotal: $1,200.00 · GST (15%): $180.00 · **Total: $1,380.00**
**Why:** Business clients can claim back GST input credits — they want to see the amounts separately

### GST Inclusive
**Best for:** Quoting homeowners and private clients
**Format:** Total: $1,380.00 (GST included)
**Why:** Simpler for clients who can't claim GST. Clearer total amount upfront.

### The rule
If you're GST-registered (over $60,000 turnover), you **must** show GST separately on tax invoices. Quotes can be either format — just be consistent and clear.

## Tips for Winning More Plumbing Jobs with Your Quotes

**1. Quote on the same day**
NZ homeowners and project managers often get 3–5 quotes. The first professional quote that arrives usually wins — price is secondary to speed and professionalism.

**2. Be specific about scope**
Vague quotes ("plumbing work — $X") lead to disputes. Itemise clearly. Clients trust plumbers who explain what they're doing.

**3. Include a validity period**
Material prices move. A quote without an expiry can come back to bite you 3 months later when copper prices have jumped.

**5. Send in a format the client can open**
PDF is the safest format. Avoid sending a Word document — formatting breaks across devices. A professional PDF shows you're organised.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much should I charge per hour as a plumber in NZ?
NZ plumber hourly rates in 2026 range from $80–$130/hour for a qualified plumber. In Auckland and Wellington, rates tend to be at the higher end. Regional areas (Canterbury, Waikato) typically run $80–$110/hour. Always factor in your overhead costs, insurance, and tool costs when setting your rate.

### Do I need to include my NZBN on a quote?
A New Zealand Business Number (NZBN) is not legally required on a quote, but it adds credibility and is required on formal tax invoices. If you're GST-registered, you must include your GST registration number on any invoice over $50.

### What's the difference between a quote and an estimate?
In NZ, a **quote** is a fixed price — once accepted, you're bound to that price (with reasonable variation for unforeseen work). An **estimate** is a rough guide and can change. Always be clear on your document which type you're providing to avoid disputes.

### How long should a plumbing quote be valid for?
30 days is standard in NZ. For large jobs (full bathroom, new build), 14 days is reasonable given material price volatility. Always include the expiry date clearly on the quote.

### Should I charge for a quote?
Most residential plumbers in NZ offer free quotes for standard jobs. For large scopes (full renovations, new builds), charging a site visit fee ($80–$150) is acceptable and increasingly common. If you charge, state it clearly upfront.

## GST on {{Trade}} Quotes

If you're GST-registered (over $60,000 annual turnover), you must show GST on all invoices over $50. For quotes, both GST-inclusive and GST-exclusive formats are acceptable — but state clearly which applies.

- **GST-exclusive** (+ GST): Better for business/commercial clients who claim GST
- **GST-inclusive**: Better for homeowner clients — simpler, total amount shown upfront

Quick calculation:
- Add GST: amount × 1.15 = GST-inclusive total
- Remove GST: GST-inclusive amount ÷ 1.15 = GST-exclusive amount

Use our [NZ GST calculator](/tools/gst-calculator) for quick calculations.
