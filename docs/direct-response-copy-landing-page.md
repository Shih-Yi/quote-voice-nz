## Review Notes

### A/B Test Priority (Landing Page)

1. **Headline:** Test Option A ("60 Seconds. From Your Ute.") vs Option B ("Still Doing Quotes at 9pm?") — this is the single highest-leverage test
2. **CTA button text:** "Get Early Access — Free" vs "Join the Waitlist"
3. **Hero subheadline:** With "No signal needed" mention vs without

### A/B Test Priority (Ads)

1. **Ad 1 (Problem-Agitate) vs Ad 2 (Before/After)** — test which framing drives more clicks
2. **Headline test:** "60-Second Quotes" vs "Speak It. Send It. Sorted."

### Checklist Watch Items

- **Proof gap:** Pre-launch = no testimonials. Prioritise getting 3-5 real tradie quotes within the first 2 weeks of early access. Replace the "Built by Kiwis" section with real proof ASAP
- **60-second claim:** Must be verifiable. If real-world usage averages 90-120 seconds, adjust to "under two minutes" — credibility > aspiration
- **Kiwi tone calibration:** Have 2-3 actual NZ tradies read the landing page copy. If anything feels "too American" or "too salesy," tone it down. Kiwis have excellent BS detectors

### What NOT to Do

- No countdown timers or fake scarcity on the waitlist
- No "ONLY 47 SPOTS LEFT" — Kiwis will see through it instantly
- No exclamation marks in headlines — the copy should feel confident, not breathless
- No stock photos of tradies — use real product screenshots or nothing
