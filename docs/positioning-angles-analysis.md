## The 7 Angles

### Lens 1: The Transformation

**Angle 1A: "From quoting at 9pm to quoting on-site"**
- Before: Tradie works all day, then sits at a desk for 2 hours at night writing quotes, sacrificing family time
- After: Quote sent in 60 seconds before driving away from the job site

**Angle 1B: "From 'I'll send you a quote later' to 'It's already on your phone, mate'"**
- Before: Client waits 3-5 days for a quote -> finds someone else
- After: Quote delivered on the spot -> first to quote wins

### Lens 2: The Enemy

**Angle 2A: "Apps that need you at a desk weren't built for tradies"**
- Enemy = Tradify / Fergus / ServiceM8 — tools that require learning a new system and sitting down to operate
- Tradify admits it needs "free training sessions" — if you need training, it's not simple enough

**Angle 2B: "Paper quotes and Excel are costing you jobs"**
- Enemy = Old ways (paper, Excel, handwritten quotes)
- Data point: First to quote wins 60%+ of jobs

### Lens 3: The Unique Mechanism

**Angle 3A: "The Chur Method(TM) — Speak, Confirm, Send"**
- Named three-step process: Speak -> Confirm -> Send
- Complete quote in under 30 seconds
- AI understands "two grand for the hot water cylinder, plus labour" in Kiwi speak

**Angle 3B: "Zero-Bar Quoting(TM) — Quote with zero signal"**
- Works in Canterbury mountains, West Coast remote sites
- IndexedDB offline storage -> auto-sync when signal returns
- This is a **technical moat** — competitors can't replicate easily

### Lens 4: The Niche Claim

**Angle 4A: "The only voice quoting tool for NZ tradies"**
- Ultra-precise niche: NZ + Tradies + Voice-to-Quote
- 15% GST auto-calculation, NZD currency, NZ spelling (Labour not Labor)
- Placemakers / Mitre 10 price integration

**Angle 4B: "Built for one-man-band Kiwi tradies"**
- NZ has ~70,000 sole trader tradies
- No office, no assistant, no time to learn complex systems
- They don't need a "job management platform" — they need to "get the quote out fast"

### Lens 5: The Speed / Simplicity Claim

**Angle 5A: "60-second quotes. For real."**
- Tradify quoting: Open app -> select client -> new quote -> add line items -> calculate GST -> send (~15-30 min)
- ChurQuote: Tap -> speak -> confirm -> send (~60 sec)
- Speed difference = 15-30x

**Angle 5B: "If you can send a voice message, you can use ChurQuote"**
- Zero learning curve, no training needed
- As simple as sending a WhatsApp voice note
- Your dad could use it

### Lens 6: The Social Proof Angle

**Angle 6A: "Canterbury rural tradies finally have a tool that works"**
- Existing products require stable internet = excludes massive rural tradie population
- First user stories from remote areas will be extremely compelling

**Angle 6B: "Save X hours per month on quoting alone"**
- Tradify claims "save 10+ hours/week on admin" but includes all management time
- ChurQuote can quantify precisely: "Each quote from 20 min -> 1 min = XX hours saved per month"

### Lens 7: The Belief Reframe

**Angle 7A: "You don't need Job Management Software. You need to quote faster."**
- False belief: "I need a full job management system"
- Truth: For most sole trader tradies, quoting speed is the bottleneck. 80% of "admin pain" comes from the quoting phase.
- Why pay $50/month/user for a system you only use 20% of?

**Angle 7B: "Voice quoting isn't professional? Wrong. Slow is unprofessional."**
- False belief: "Voice-generated quotes won't look formal enough"
- Truth: AI-produced quotes are more professional than handwritten ones, and clients care about speed and accuracy

## Expanded Angles

### #1 Winner: "60-Second Quotes" (Angle 5A)

**One-liner positioning statement:**
> NZ tradies use ChurQuote to send professional quotes in 60 seconds by voice — no typing, no laptop, no signal required.

**The 3-sentence pitch:**
1. You spend all day on the tools, then another 2 hours at night on Tradify typing up quotes. That's not your job.
2. ChurQuote lets you speak for 60 seconds and AI produces a professional quote with GST calculations and Placemakers pricing.
3. Quote sent before you leave the job site. First to quote wins. Simple as.

**Headline variations:**

1. **The Specificity Headline:**
   > "A 20-minute quoting process, compressed to 60 seconds. The voice quoting tool for NZ tradies."

2. **The Contrast Headline:**
   > "Tradify needs you to sit down and type. ChurQuote just needs you to speak."

3. **The Audience-First Headline:**
   > "Kiwi Tradie — how much time do you waste on quotes every day? Try 60 seconds."

**The objection it pre-empts:**
"I don't have time to learn new software" — The 60-second promise directly eliminates the fear of "yet another system to learn." If the whole thing takes 60 seconds, the perceived learning cost is zero.

**Where it works best:**
- **Primary:** Facebook/Instagram short video ads (real demo of 60-second quoting process)
- **Secondary:** Trade show live demonstrations (let tradies try it on the spot)
- **Content:** YouTube "60-second challenge" video series

## Final Recommendation

### Primary Angle: "60-Second Quotes"

**Why this angle wins:**

1. **Broadest appeal** — Every tradie hates the hassle of quoting, whether city or rural, large company or one-man-band
2. **Highest verifiability** — You can film a 60-second video showing the entire process. Customers see fact, not promise
3. **Maximum emotional impact** — The "60 seconds" vs "20 minutes" contrast needs zero explanation to land
4. **Competitors cannot follow** — Tradify/Fergus's entire business model is built on "full-featured job management." They cannot reposition as "60-second quoting"

**#1 Risk:**
If AI parsing accuracy for Kiwi accents falls below 90%, "60 seconds" becomes "60 seconds + 5 minutes of corrections." First-user experience will determine whether this angle lives or dies.

**Smallest Possible Experiment (First Test):**
1. Film 3 real demo videos (plumber, electrician, landscaper — one each), each showing a complete quote in 60 seconds
2. Run Facebook ads targeting NZ tradies ($500 budget, 2 weeks)
3. Target metrics: CTR > 3%, waitlist signups > 100
4. If CTR < 1.5%, switch to Angle 4A (NZ-only voice quoting) for testing

**Secondary Angle (Reserve):**
"The Only Voice Quoting Tool for NZ Tradies" — If "60 seconds" feels too generic and lacks differentiation, switch to the NZ localisation niche claim. These two angles can be combined:

> **Combined Tagline:**
> **"60-second voice quotes. Built for Kiwi Tradies. Works with zero signal."**
>
> — One line containing Speed (60 sec) + Niche (Kiwi Tradies) + Mechanism (zero signal)

---

**Bottom line:** Your real competitor isn't Tradify or Fergus — your real competitor is the habit of "I'll do the quote when I get home tonight." The fastest way to break that habit is to let a tradie see with their own eyes that 60 seconds is all it takes. Show, don't tell.
