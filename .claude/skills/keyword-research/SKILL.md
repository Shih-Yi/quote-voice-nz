# Keyword Research - SEO Opportunity Discovery Engine

Systematic keyword research framework for finding high-intent, low-competition opportunities — especially programmatic SEO patterns that can be templated and scaled.

---

## Philosophy

Most keyword research produces a spreadsheet of 500 keywords that never gets executed. This skill produces:

1. **Actionable clusters** — grouped by intent and template-ability
2. **Programmatic patterns** — repeatable keyword structures that scale to hundreds of pages
3. **Priority ranking** — what to build first based on intent + competition + volume
4. **Content specs** — what each page needs to rank, not just which keyword to target

---

## Phase 1: Seed Discovery

### 1A: Core Seed Keywords

Start from the product/service and extract seed keywords across these categories:

| Category | Pattern | Example (Quote Tool for NZ Tradies) |
|----------|---------|-------------------------------------|
| **Product** | [what you are] | quote software, quoting app, quoting tool |
| **Problem** | [pain you solve] | slow quoting, lost quotes, manual quoting |
| **Audience** | [who you serve] + [need] | tradie quoting, plumber invoicing, electrician quotes |
| **Alternative** | [competitor] + alternative | Tradify alternative, Fergus alternative, Jobber alternative |
| **Comparison** | [product A] vs [product B] | Tradify vs Fergus, quote software comparison nz |
| **How-to** | how to [task you simplify] | how to write a trade quote, how to quote a plumbing job |
| **Location** | [service] + [location] | quote software nz, tradie app new zealand |
| **Long-tail** | [specific use case] | voice to quote app, quote from phone tradie |

### 1B: Audience Language Mining

Find the words real people use (not marketing language):

- **Sources to mine:** Trade forums, Reddit (r/diynz, r/newzealand), Facebook trade groups, Google autocomplete, "People also ask", Google related searches, competitor reviews
- **What to capture:** The exact phrases people type when describing the problem, the slang and abbreviations they use, questions they ask, complaints about existing solutions

### 1C: Competitor Keyword Gaps

Identify keywords competitors rank for that you don't:

- **Direct competitors:** List their domains
- **Content competitors:** Blogs/resources that rank for your keywords but don't sell competing products
- **Gap analysis:** Keywords where competitors rank page 1 but their content is thin, outdated, or poorly targeted

## Phase 2: Keyword Expansion

### 2A: Modifier Expansion

Take each seed keyword and expand with proven modifier patterns:

| Modifier Type | Pattern | Examples |
|---------------|---------|----------|
| **Intent** | best, top, free, cheap, professional | best quote software nz |
| **Comparison** | vs, alternative, compared to, or | Tradify vs Fergus |
| **Time** | 2026, this year, today, fast | best quoting app 2026 |
| **Audience** | for [trade type], for small business, for contractors | quote app for plumbers |
| **Location** | nz, new zealand, auckland, christchurch | tradie software nz |
| **Format** | template, example, sample, pdf, free | plumbing quote template nz |
| **Question** | how to, what is, why, when to, how much | how to quote a building job nz |
| **Feature** | with [feature], [feature] included | quoting app with gst calculator |

### 2B: Programmatic SEO Patterns

Identify repeatable keyword structures that can generate many pages from one template:

**Pattern format:** `[Variable A] + [Constant] + [Variable B]`

**Examples for a NZ trade quote tool:**

| Pattern | Variable A | Constant | Variable B | Page Count |
|---------|-----------|----------|------------|------------|
| `[trade] quote template nz` | plumber, electrician, painter, landscaper... | quote template | nz | 20+ |
| `how to quote a [job type]` | bathroom renovation, kitchen remodel, rewire... | how to quote a | - | 30+ |
| `[trade] hourly rate nz [year]` | plumber, electrician, builder... | hourly rate nz | 2026 | 20+ |
| `[trade] invoice template nz` | plumber, electrician, painter... | invoice template nz | - | 20+ |
| `cost of [service] nz` | bathroom renovation, house rewire, deck build... | cost of... nz | - | 50+ |

**Evaluation criteria for programmatic patterns:**
- Can we generate 10+ unique pages from this pattern?
- Does each page have distinct, valuable content (not just variable swaps)?
- Is there real search volume across the variations?
- Can we provide genuinely useful information on each page?

## Phase 3: Intent Classification

Classify every keyword by search intent:

| Intent | Signal Words | Buyer Journey Stage | Content Type |
|--------|-------------|-------------------|--------------|
| **Informational** | how to, what is, why, guide, tips | Awareness | Blog post, guide, video |
| **Commercial Investigation** | best, top, review, comparison, vs, alternative | Consideration | Comparison page, review, listicle |
| **Transactional** | buy, pricing, sign up, free trial, download | Decision | Landing page, pricing page, CTA page |
| **Navigational** | [brand name], [brand] login, [brand] pricing | Retention | Brand pages, documentation |

**Priority rule:** For growth, prioritise in this order:
1. **Commercial Investigation** — Highest conversion potential, often underserved
2. **Transactional** — Direct revenue keywords, but often high competition
3. **Informational** — Traffic building, authority, top-of-funnel
4. **Navigational** — Only relevant once you have brand awareness

## Phase 4: Keyword Clustering

Group keywords into topic clusters that map to single pages or page groups:

### Cluster Format

```
### Cluster: [Topic Name]
- **Primary keyword:** [Highest volume keyword in the cluster]
- **Secondary keywords:** [Related keywords this page should also target]
- **Search intent:** [Informational / Commercial / Transactional]
- **Estimated combined volume:** [Sum of all keywords in cluster]
- **Competition level:** [Low / Medium / High]
- **Content type:** [Blog / Landing page / Tool page / Comparison page]
- **Programmatic?:** [Yes — pattern: X / No — unique content needed]
```

### Clustering Rules
- Keywords with the same intent AND same ideal content format belong in one cluster
- Don't cluster keywords that need fundamentally different pages
- One page per cluster — don't cannibalise your own rankings
- A cluster should have one clear primary keyword and 3-10 supporting keywords

---

## Phase 5: Opportunity Scoring

Score each cluster on these dimensions (1-5):

| Dimension | Question |
|-----------|----------|
| **Volume** | Is there meaningful search volume? (Even 50/month matters in NZ) |
| **Intent alignment** | Does this searcher want what we offer? |
| **Competition** | Can we realistically rank on page 1 within 6 months? |
| **Content feasibility** | Can we create genuinely better content than what exists? |
| **Conversion potential** | If they land on our page, how likely are they to convert? |
| **Scalability** | Is this a one-off page or part of a programmatic pattern? |

**Priority tiers:**
- **Tier 1 (Ship now):** Score 25+ AND high conversion potential — these are money pages
- **Tier 2 (Ship next):** Score 20-24 — solid opportunities, schedule within 30 days
- **Tier 3 (Backlog):** Score 15-19 — good for authority building, do when resources allow
- **Below 15:** Skip unless it's a strategic brand play

## Phase 6: Content Requirements

For each Tier 1 cluster, specify:

```
## Page: [Primary Keyword]
### SEO Spec
- **Target keyword:** [Primary]
- **Secondary keywords:** [3-5 to weave in naturally]
- **Title tag:** [60 chars max, keyword-front-loaded]
- **Meta description:** [155 chars max, includes CTA]
- **URL slug:** /[clean-keyword-slug]
- **H1:** [May differ from title tag for readability]

### Content Spec
- **Word count target:** [Based on competing pages analysis]
- **Content format:** [Listicle / Guide / Comparison / Tool / Template]
- **Key sections (H2s):**
  - [Section 1]
  - [Section 2]
  - [Section 3]
- **Must-include elements:**
  - [ ] NZ-specific data or context
  - [ ] Practical examples
  - [ ] Internal link to [product/pricing page]
  - [ ] CTA placement (above fold + end of content)
- **Differentiator:** [What makes our page better than current #1 result]

### Technical SEO
- **Schema markup:** [FAQ, HowTo, Product, LocalBusiness — whichever applies]
- **Internal links from:** [Existing pages that should link to this]
- **Internal links to:** [Pages this should link to]
```

## Phase 7: Programmatic SEO Blueprint

If programmatic patterns were identified, produce a template specification:

```
## Programmatic Template: [Pattern Name]
### Pattern
- URL: /[constant]/[variable-slug]
- Example: /quote-template/plumber-nz

### Variable Data
- **Variable:** [e.g. trade type]
- **Values:** [List all variations to generate]
- **Data source:** [Where the unique content per page comes from]

### Template Structure
- **H1:** [Template with variable] — e.g. "Free [Trade] Quote Template NZ"
- **Intro paragraph:** [Personalised to the trade, 2-3 sentences]
- **Section 1:** [Common items quoted for this trade]
- **Section 2:** [Average rates in NZ for this trade]
- **Section 3:** [Tips for quoting this type of work]
- **CTA:** [Bridge to product]
- **Dynamic elements:** [What changes per page vs. what stays constant]

### Quality Guardrails
- Each page MUST have at least [X] words of unique content
- No thin pages — if we can't provide real value for a variation, don't generate it
- Include NZ-specific data points per variation where possible
- Human review required before publishing batch
```

## Key Principles

- **NZ search volumes are small — and that's fine.** 50 searches/month in NZ with high intent beats 10,000 generic global searches. Small volumes with perfect intent convert.
- **Programmatic SEO is the multiplier.** One template that generates 20 pages beats writing 20 individual blog posts.
- **Intent over volume.** A keyword with 20 searches/month and transactional intent is worth more than 2,000 searches/month informational.
- **Steal the language, not the strategy.** Use your audience's exact words as keywords. "Tradie quoting app" not "field service management solution."
- **Every page needs a job.** If a page doesn't have a clear conversion action, it's a vanity metric. Tie every keyword cluster to a business outcome.
- **Local wins.** "[Service] nz" keywords are lower competition and higher relevance. Always include location variants.
