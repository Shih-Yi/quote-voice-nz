# DTC Ad - Performance Advertising Strategy Engine

Build full-funnel paid advertising strategies using battle-tested DTC (Direct-to-Consumer) ad frameworks — adapted for SaaS, services, and local market contexts like NZ.

DTC brands spend millions testing what works in paid social. This skill extracts those learnings and applies them to any product or service — without the DTC budget.

---

## Why DTC Logic Works Beyond E-Commerce

DTC brands have pressure-tested every variable in paid advertising:
- Hook timing (first 3 seconds)
- Thumb-stopping creative patterns
- Offer structuring and risk reversal
- Retargeting sequences and frequency
- Creative fatigue management
- Attribution and testing methodology

These principles are universal. A tradie app ad follows the same attention economics as a skincare brand ad — the human scrolling past is the same person.

## Phase 1: Advertising Context

Extract or confirm:

- **Product/service:** What are we advertising?
- **Price point:** Determines funnel depth (low price = direct CTA, high price = nurture)
- **Target audience:** Demographics, psychographics, pain points
- **Conversion goal:** Sign-up, free trial, purchase, lead capture, app install
- **Budget range:** Determines channel selection and testing velocity
- **Existing assets:** Landing page, testimonials, product screenshots, video footage?
- **Platforms:** Facebook/Instagram, Google, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn?
- **Geographic target:** NZ-wide, specific regions, global?
- **Previous ad performance:** What's been tested? What worked/failed?

## Phase 2: Full-Funnel Architecture

### The 3-Stage Funnel

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  STAGE 1: COLD TRAFFIC (Top of Funnel)  │
│  Goal: Awareness + Interest             │
│  Audience: Broad / Lookalike / Interest  │
│  Creative: Hook-heavy, problem-aware     │
│  CTA: Soft (Learn more, Watch, Read)     │
│  Budget allocation: 60%                  │
└──────────────────┬──────────────────────┘
                   │
┌──────────────────▼──────────────────────┐
│  STAGE 2: WARM TRAFFIC (Middle)         │
│  Goal: Consideration + Trust            │
│  Audience: Retarget engagers/visitors    │
│  Creative: Proof-heavy, mechanism-aware  │
│  CTA: Medium (Try free, See pricing)     │
│  Budget allocation: 25%                  │
└──────────────────┬──────────────────────┘
                   │
┌──────────────────▼──────────────────────┐
│  STAGE 3: HOT TRAFFIC (Bottom)          │
│  Goal: Conversion + Action              │
│  Audience: Retarget visitors/cart/trial  │
│  Creative: Offer-heavy, urgency, proof   │
│  CTA: Hard (Start now, Sign up, Buy)     │
│  Budget allocation: 15%                  │
└─────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Audience Definitions

For each funnel stage, define:

```
### Stage [N] Audiences
| Audience Name | Type | Definition | Estimated Size |
|---------------|------|-----------|----------------|
| [Name] | [Cold/Warm/Hot] | [Targeting criteria] | [Estimate] |
```

**Cold audience types:**
- Interest-based (trade-related interests, tool brands, industry publications)
- Lookalike (1%, 2%, 5% based on existing customers or email list)
- Broad (age + gender + location only — let the algorithm find them)
- Keyword-based (Google Search, YouTube intent)

**Warm audience types:**
- Website visitors (last 30/60/90 days)
- Video viewers (25%, 50%, 75% completion)
- Social engagers (liked, commented, saved, shared)
- Lead magnet downloaders / quiz completers
- Email list (non-customers)

**Hot audience types:**
- Pricing page visitors
- Free trial users (not converted)
- Cart/checkout abandoners
- Past customers (upsell/cross-sell)

## Phase 3: Creative Strategy

### 3A: The DTC Creative Framework

Every ad creative needs:

1. **Hook (0-3 seconds):** Stop the scroll. This is 80% of the ad's job
2. **Problem (3-8 seconds):** Name the pain. Make them feel it
3. **Solution (8-15 seconds):** Introduce the product as the answer
4. **Proof (15-25 seconds):** Show it works. Testimonials, demo, data
5. **CTA (last 3-5 seconds):** Tell them exactly what to do next

### 3B: Hook Library

Generate 10+ hooks across these proven categories:

| Hook Type | Formula | Example |
|-----------|---------|---------|
| **Problem call-out** | "Still [doing painful thing]?" | "Still writing quotes at the kitchen table at 9pm?" |
| **Bold claim** | "[Specific result] in [timeframe]" | "Send a professional quote in 60 seconds flat" |
| **Social proof** | "[Number] [audience] already [result]" | "1,200+ Kiwi tradies stopped losing jobs to slow quotes" |
| **Curiosity** | "The [audience] secret to [result]" | "How one sparky doubled his quote win rate with his phone" |
| **Contrarian** | "Stop [common advice]. Here's why..." | "Stop spending hours on quote templates. There's a faster way" |
| **Us vs Them** | "While other [audience] [old way]..." | "While other tradies are writing quotes by hand..." |
| **Question** | "What if you could [desire] without [pain]?" | "What if you could quote a job from your ute in 60 seconds?" |
| **Before/After** | "I used to [pain]. Now I [result]" | "I used to lose 5 hours a week on quotes. Now it takes 5 minutes" |
| **Specific number** | "[Exact number] [audience] [did thing]" | "347 NZ plumbers switched to voice quoting last month" |
| **Pattern interrupt** | Unexpected visual or statement | [Shows someone speaking into phone, quote appears on screen] |

### 3C: Creative Formats

For each funnel stage, specify which formats to produce:

| Format | Best For | Specs |
|--------|----------|-------|
| **Static image** | Quick tests, simple messages | 1080x1080 (feed), 1080x1920 (story) |
| **Carousel** | Feature walkthroughs, testimonials, before/after | 3-5 slides, 1080x1080 |
| **Short video (15s)** | Hook + CTA, retargeting | 9:16 (story/reels), 1:1 (feed), 16:9 (YouTube) |
| **Medium video (30-60s)** | Problem-solution-proof, demos | All aspect ratios |
| **UGC-style** | Trust building, authenticity | Shot on phone, natural lighting, real person |
| **Screen recording** | Product demos, feature highlights | With voiceover or text overlay |
| **Testimonial** | Social proof, warm/hot retargeting | Real customer, specific result |

## Phase 6: NZ Market Adaptations

- **Small market reality:** NZ has ~5M people. Audience sizes are small. Don't over-segment cold audiences — 50K-200K is fine
- **Platform priority:** Facebook/Instagram dominant for tradies. Google Search for high-intent. TikTok growing but less proven for B2B
- **Tone:** Understated wins. Kiwi scepticism is real. "Pretty bloody good" > "The #1 Revolutionary Platform"
- **UGC style:** Authentic, on-site footage resonates. Polished studio ads feel out of place for trade audiences
- **Pricing in ads:** Always NZD. Include GST context. "From $X/month + GST" is clearer than hiding it
- **Testimonials:** Use real Kiwi names, locations, and trades. "Dave, Plumber, Christchurch" builds trust. Generic stock-photo testimonials destroy it
- **Seasons:** Construction/trade activity peaks Sept-April in NZ. Budget accordingly. Winter is maintenance/planning season — different messaging
- **Frequency caution:** Small audiences fatigue fast. Refresh creative every 2-3 weeks. Monitor frequency metrics closely
- **Compliance:** Follow NZ Advertising Standards Authority (ASA) rules. No misleading claims. Substantiate any specific results claimed

## Key Principles

- **The hook is the ad.** If the first 3 seconds don't stop the scroll, nothing else matters. Spend 80% of creative energy on hooks.
- **Test creative, not audiences.** In 2026, platform algorithms find the right people. Your job is to give them creative that converts.
- **Ugly ads often win.** UGC-style, phone-shot, raw content frequently outperforms polished studio creative. Authenticity > production value.
- **Frequency kills.** In small NZ audiences, creative fatigue hits fast. Plan for 2-3 new creative sets per month minimum.
- **One message per ad.** Don't try to communicate 5 benefits. One hook, one proof point, one CTA.
- **Retargeting is where money is made.** Cold traffic builds the pipeline. Retargeting converts it. Never run cold without retargeting in place.
- **Data beats opinions.** Run the test. Let the numbers decide. Kill losers fast ($20-30 spend with no signal = kill). Scale winners gradually (20% budget increase per 3 days).
